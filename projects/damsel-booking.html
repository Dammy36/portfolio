Overview

The booking side of Damsel Nails. Clients browse the salon's real services and prices, pick a date and time, and send their details — inside a dashboard-style app with its own pages for appointments, services, nail artists, gallery, reviews, profile and settings.

It works alongside the Damsel Nails website.

02

Key features

  • Three-step booking flow — Choose Service, Date & Time, Your Details — with a live “Your Booking” summary and total.
  • The salon's real services and prices in naira, with how long each one takes.
  • 10% off a client's first booking, shown as a “New Client” offer.
  • Gallery photos loaded from Supabase Storage, with a local fallback and a lightbox.
  • An AI Style Advisor and a chat widget, both routed through n8n.
  • A small Express server that forwards new bookings to an n8n webhook.
  • An admin dashboard backed by Supabase.
